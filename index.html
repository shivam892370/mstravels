Journey Itinerary

Day 1: Yamunotri

Begin your journey with the sacred Yamunotri temple, the source of the Yamuna river.

Day 2: Gangotri

Visit the holy Gangotri temple, where the Ganges river originates.

Day 3: Kedarnath

Pay homage at Kedarnath, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva.

Day 4: Badrinath

Complete your journey at Badrinath, the abode of Lord Vishnu.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit Char Dham?

The best time to visit Char Dham is from May to October, when the weather is pleasant and the temples are open for pilgrims.

What documents are required for the journey?

You need to carry a valid ID proof, medical certificate, and registration documents for the journey.

Is there any age restriction for the journey?

There is no specific age restriction, but pilgrims should be physically fit to undertake the journey.
